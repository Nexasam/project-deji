VERIFIED SHORTLET — CLIENT GO-LIVE TESTING GUIDE
=================================================

Purpose
-------

This guide helps the Verified Shortlet team test the complete platform journey before go-live.

It is written for the client and client testing team. It does not contain server commands, secret keys, deployment instructions or technical implementation details.

Important responsibility boundary
---------------------------------

Two separated platform-owner accounts manage and protect Verified Shortlet: the primary platform owner and the settlement approver. Neither account creates a live host's business, property, staff or bookings.

The correct responsibility flow is:

1. The primary platform owner configures and supervises the platform.
2. The business owner or property host registers independently.
3. The business owner completes business onboarding.
4. The platform owner reviews and approves or rejects the business.
5. The business owner creates and submits properties.
6. The platform owner reviews and publishes or rejects properties.
7. Guests register, verify their identity and make bookings.
8. The business owner manages staff, operations and guest stays.

The optional Presentation Sandbox is different. It contains disposable records created for demonstrations. Every sandbox property is clearly marked “Test property” and must not be treated as a live host or property.


BEFORE THE TEST
===============

Prepare five separate browser sessions or devices:

Browser Session A: Platform owner
Browser Session B: Business/property owner
Browser Session C: Guest
Browser Session D: Cleaner or another staff member
Browser Session E: Settlement approver

Do not use one browser session for every account. Separate sessions make permissions and notifications easier to verify.

Before starting, the testing coordinator should confirm:

[ ] The staging or production URL opens securely.
[ ] Both platform-owner accounts can sign in using separate credentials.
[ ] Email delivery is working.
[ ] Payment Test mode or Live mode has been selected by the platform owner.
[ ] Test email addresses and phone numbers are available.
[ ] Every tester understands their assigned role.
[ ] No passwords or secret provider keys will be placed in screenshots or shared messages.


PART 1 — PLATFORM-OWNER PREPARATION
===================================

Who performs this: Primary platform owner

1. Sign in through the platform administration login page.
2. Open Platform Configuration.
3. Confirm the correct payment environment is selected.
4. Confirm the required payment providers are enabled.
5. Confirm email delivery is enabled and send a test email.
6. Confirm identity verification is configured for the current test environment.
7. Review platform fees and installment-payment settings.
8. Review notification and calendar synchronization settings.
9. Confirm the platform audit page is available.

Expected result:

[ ] Configuration saves successfully.
[ ] The test email is received.
[ ] Only the platform owner can access sensitive platform configuration.
[ ] Guests and business owners cannot see provider credentials or environment controls.


PART 2 — BUSINESS-OWNER REGISTRATION
====================================

Who performs this: Prospective business/property owner

1. Open the registration page in Browser Session B.
2. Enter the owner's name, email address, phone number and password.
3. Choose the business/property-host registration path.
4. Submit the registration.
5. Open the verification email and verify the email address.
6. Continue to business onboarding.
7. Enter the business or trading name.
8. Enter the primary contact information.
9. Enter the business address and business type.
10. Provide the available registration details.
11. Enter the business administrator's 11-digit NIN and BVN.
12. Read and accept the identity-verification consent statement.
13. Submit the business for review. The identity provider runs the checks during submission.

Expected result:

[ ] The owner can register without platform-owner assistance.
[ ] The email-verification message is delivered.
[ ] The new business appears in the owner's workspace.
[ ] The business shows a pending-review status.
[ ] The owner cannot approve their own business.
[ ] Only masked NIN/BVN digits, provider references and results are retained; full numbers are not displayed to platform administrators.


PART 3 — PLATFORM BUSINESS VERIFICATION
=======================================

Who performs this: Platform owner

1. Return to Browser Session A.
2. Open Businesses.
3. Select the newly registered business.
4. Review the submitted business details and contact information.
5. Review the property owner or business host's NIN and BVN verification results returned by the configured identity provider.
6. Approve or reject the business and provide a clear reason.
7. Open the audit history and confirm the decision was recorded.

Important:

The identity provider verifies NIN and BVN during onboarding. The platform owner reviews the masked results and provider references as part of the wider business-approval decision. The platform owner cannot manually mark NIN or BVN as verified.

Expected result:

[ ] The approved business becomes Verified.
[ ] A rejected business receives a clear reason.
[ ] The decision records the reviewing platform owner, time and reason.
[ ] Property approval remains a separate process.
[ ] Payout remains unavailable until all required identity and bank-account checks pass.


PART 4 — PROPERTY CREATION
==========================

Who performs this: Business owner only

Initial property creation and submission must be performed strictly by the business owner—not by staff or the platform owner.

1. Return to Browser Session B.
2. Open Properties and select Add property.
3. Enter the complete property name and address.
4. Select the property type.
5. Enter capacity, bedrooms, beds and bathrooms.
6. Add amenities and inventory information.
7. Set check-in and checkout times.
8. Add house rules.
9. Add arrival and key-collection instructions.
10. Set the nightly price.
11. Add any longer-stay promotion or eligible date-range offer.
12. Upload property photographs and requested documents.
13. Paste an optional YouTube walkthrough link. Use a public or unlisted video that the host is authorized to share; do not upload the video file to Verified Shortlet.
14. Add the public marketplace title, summary and description.
15. Add an external calendar connection if available.
16. Preview the property and confirm the YouTube video embeds correctly when supplied.
17. Submit it for platform review.

Expected result:

[ ] All property sections save correctly.
[ ] Input fields and uploads work on desktop and mobile.
[ ] A valid YouTube URL embeds correctly, and an invalid/non-YouTube URL is rejected clearly.
[ ] The property shows Pending review.
[ ] The property is not publicly bookable before approval.
[ ] The property owner cannot approve or publish their own property.


PART 5 — PROPERTY REVIEW AND PUBLICATION
========================================

Who performs this: Platform owner

1. Return to Browser Session A.
2. Open Properties.
3. Select the newly submitted property.
4. Review its address, images, documents, capacity and description.
5. Review pricing, promotions, check-in/checkout times and house rules.
6. Review calendar and availability information.
7. Approve and publish the property, or reject it with a correction reason.
8. Enable the Super host badge only when the platform's qualification requirements have been met.

Expected result:

[ ] Approved property appears on the marketplace.
[ ] Rejected property displays a useful reason to the business owner.
[ ] No generic Verified watermark appears on marketplace property cards.
[ ] Super host appears only when enabled by the platform owner.
[ ] A Test property badge appears only on Presentation Sandbox properties.


PART 6 — GUEST REGISTRATION AND IDENTITY
========================================

Who performs this: Guest

1. Open the registration page in Browser Session C.
2. Register as an individual guest.
3. Verify the email address.
4. Open Guest Settings.
5. Enter the requested NIN information.
6. Select Verify.
7. Confirm the identity status updates successfully.
8. Configure notification preferences using the on/off controls.

Expected result:

[ ] Guest registration succeeds.
[ ] Email verification succeeds.
[ ] NIN verification gives clear success or failure feedback.
[ ] An unverified guest cannot proceed with payment where identity is required.
[ ] Notification preferences save successfully.


PART 7 — MARKETPLACE, SHARE AND FAVOURITES
==========================================

Who performs this: Guest

1. Open the marketplace.
2. Search by location, date and number of guests.
3. Confirm the search section remains accessible while scrolling.
4. Browse horizontal property sections on desktop and mobile.
5. Open the new property.
6. Confirm the full property address, host, amenities, capacity and house rules appear.
7. Select Share.
8. Select Save.
9. Open Favourites and confirm the property appears.
10. Remove it from Favourites and confirm the count updates without navigating away.

Expected result:

[ ] Search results match the selected filters.
[ ] Property cards remain consistent in size and alignment.
[ ] Share opens the device share option or copies the property URL.
[ ] Save and Remove work without opening the property unintentionally.
[ ] The favourite count updates immediately.


PART 8 — AVAILABILITY AND PRICING
=================================

Who performs this: Guest

1. Open the property's availability calendar.
2. Confirm booked dates are clearly unavailable.
3. Confirm available dates are clearly shown.
4. Select a check-in date.
5. Select a checkout date.
6. Change only the checkout date and confirm the check-in date remains selected.
7. Change the check-in date without reloading the page.
8. Confirm the price updates as dates change.
9. Confirm eligible longer-stay discounts appear automatically.
10. Confirm the marketplace card's date-range offer remains visually aligned with standard nightly-price cards.

Expected result:

[ ] Date selection is smooth and changeable.
[ ] Booked dates cannot be selected.
[ ] Check-in is not unnecessarily reset when checkout changes.
[ ] Night count, discount and total update correctly.
[ ] The property details page still shows the nightly-price breakdown.


PART 9 — BOOKING AND PAYMENT
============================

Who performs this: Guest

1. Select valid check-in and checkout dates.
2. Enter adults and children.
3. Enter the guest phone number.
4. Enter an optional special request.
5. Continue to checkout.
6. Select Paystack or Flutterwave.
7. Choose full payment for the main test.
8. Accept the house rules and cancellation policy.
9. Complete payment.

Also test installment payment when enabled:

1. Choose an amount that is not below the configured minimum deposit.
2. Complete the first payment.
3. Confirm the outstanding balance and due date are visible.
4. Confirm full payment is required before check-in.

Expected result:

[ ] The booking is created once—never duplicated by repeated clicks.
[ ] A successful full payment confirms the booking.
[ ] A receipt is available.
[ ] The guest sees the amount paid and any outstanding balance.
[ ] The property owner sees the payment and owner balance.
[ ] The platform owner sees the payment and platform fee allocation.
[ ] Guest, property owner and platform owner receive the relevant notifications.
[ ] Fully paid arrival instructions become visible to the guest.


PART 10 — DOUBLE-BOOKING AND CALENDAR PROTECTION
================================================

1. Open the same property in a separate browser session.
2. Attempt to select the newly booked dates.
3. Confirm those dates are unavailable.
4. If a genuine external calendar feed is available, add it from the owner's calendar settings.
5. Run or wait for calendar synchronization.
6. Confirm the imported dates become unavailable.
7. Confirm the Verified Shortlet calendar export can be read by an external calendar platform.

Expected result:

[ ] Native bookings block overlapping bookings.
[ ] Imported calendar events block overlapping bookings.
[ ] The calendar shows one connected range for each stay rather than repeated disconnected labels.
[ ] Calendar synchronization does not expose private guest or payment information.


PART 11 — OWNER BOOKING WORKSPACE
=================================

Who performs this: Business owner

1. Open Notifications and locate the new-booking notification.
2. Open Bookings and find the booking reference.
3. Review dates, guest numbers, payment status and special request.
4. Use Messages to reply to the guest.
5. Confirm the reply appears immediately without reloading the page.
6. Confirm Call guest/host actions show only when a usable phone number exists.
7. Open Finance and confirm the gross payment, fee and owner balance.
8. Open Calendar and confirm the complete occupied range.

Expected result:

[ ] Booking, payment, messages, calendar, finance and operations refer to the same record.
[ ] Messages send without a full-page reload.
[ ] The recipient receives a new-message notification.


PART 12 — STAFF, ROLES AND PROPERTY ACCESS
==========================================

Who performs this: Business owner

1. Open Team.
2. Create or select a Cleaner role.
3. Give the role task-viewing and task-completion access.
4. Do not give the cleaner Finance access.
5. Invite a cleaner.
6. Assign the cleaner to only the property used for this test.
7. Create or review Accountant access.
8. Give the accountant appropriate Finance access without cleaning or property-operation controls.
9. Review Reception, Operations Manager, Maintenance, Inspector and Support roles.
10. Demonstrate read-only and read/write access where relevant.
11. Deactivate and reactivate a team member.

Expected result:

[ ] Roles control which functions a staff member may use.
[ ] Property assignments separately control which properties they may access.
[ ] Business dashboard analytics remain owner-only where configured.
[ ] Unauthorized direct URLs return a forbidden response.


PART 13 — CLEANING AND PRE-ARRIVAL OPERATIONS
==============================================

Who performs this: Business owner/operations manager and cleaner

1. The owner or operations manager opens Operations.
2. Locate the readiness task connected to the booking.
3. Assign it to the cleaner for the test property.
4. The cleaner signs in through Browser Session D.
5. The cleaner opens Assigned tasks.
6. Start the task.
7. Complete the checklist.
8. Add notes and evidence where required.
9. Complete the task.
10. Attempt to open Finance and unrestricted Bookings directly.

Expected result:

[ ] Cleaner sees only assigned task and property information.
[ ] Cleaner can update permitted task fields.
[ ] Cleaner cannot access Finance or unrelated bookings/properties.
[ ] The business owner can see the task progress.


PART 14 — CHECK-IN AND GUEST SUPPORT
====================================

Who performs this: Owner/reception, guest and support team

1. Confirm the booking is fully paid.
2. Confirm arrival instructions are complete.
3. Owner or reception checks the guest in.
4. Guest opens the booking and reads:
   - complete property address;
   - access and key instructions;
   - Wi-Fi or property guidance;
   - house rules;
   - emergency contact;
   - Message host and Call host actions.
5. Guest sends a message.
6. Confirm the message appears immediately without reloading.
7. Guest creates a maintenance or support request.
8. Support or operations assigns and resolves it.
9. Guest confirms the updated status.

Expected result:

[ ] Arrival information is hidden until the required payment condition is met.
[ ] Authorized staff can check the guest in.
[ ] Messages and support requests remain linked to the booking.
[ ] The guest receives clear status updates.


PART 15 — CHECKOUT, TURNOVER AND INSPECTION
===========================================

1. Owner or reception checks the guest out.
2. Record key return, property condition and handover notes.
3. Cleaner completes the turnover-cleaning task.
4. Inspector completes the inspection and evidence requirements.
5. Owner confirms the operational work is complete.

Expected result:

[ ] Property moves from Occupied to Cleaning/Inspection and then Available.
[ ] The completed stay becomes eligible for review.
[ ] Future guests can book only after the property is available again.


PART 16 — REVIEWS AND MODERATION
================================

Who performs this: Guest, business owner and platform owner

Guest:

1. Open the completed booking.
2. Submit ratings and written feedback.

Platform owner:

1. Open Reviews.
2. Review the booking and payment context.
3. Review the applicable scoring questions and result.
4. Approve, hide or reject the review with a reason.

Business owner:

1. Respond to the approved property review.
2. Submit a good, neutral or bad guest-stay review from the completed booking.

Expected result:

[ ] Guest review does not become public before platform moderation.
[ ] Approved review appears publicly.
[ ] Hidden or rejected content remains unavailable publicly.
[ ] Business-owner response remains linked to the review.
[ ] The guest-stay review is distinct from booking messages.


PART 17 — OWNER SETTLEMENT
==========================

Who performs this: Primary platform owner and settlement approver using separate sessions

1. Confirm the business is verified.
2. Confirm the property owner or business host's required identity checks have passed.
3. Confirm the settlement bank account is verified.
4. Open Settlements.
5. Review available owner-balance allocations.
6. The primary platform owner creates a settlement batch.
7. Confirm the primary platform owner cannot approve the batch they created.
8. Open Browser Session E and sign in as the separately configured settlement approver.
9. The settlement approver reviews and approves the batch.
10. Record completion with the external transfer reference.
11. Test reversal only where permitted and provide a reason.

Expected result:

[ ] Unverified businesses cannot receive settlement.
[ ] The same balance cannot be settled twice.
[ ] The settlement creator cannot approve their own batch.
[ ] A different authenticated platform owner can approve the batch.
[ ] Settlement actions record the person, time and reason.
[ ] The business owner can see the relevant balance and settlement status.


PART 18 — PLATFORM SAFETY AND ADMINISTRATION
============================================

Who performs this: Platform owner

Demonstrate:

[ ] Business suspension and reactivation.
[ ] User lock and unlock.
[ ] Property unpublish and rejection controls.
[ ] Review moderation.
[ ] Dispute management.
[ ] Platform audit history.
[ ] Notification delivery.
[ ] Maintenance status visibility.

Do not run deployment, migration or code-update actions during a client presentation unless the technical team has approved the exact action and confirmed a backup exists.


OPTIONAL — PRESENTATION SANDBOX
===============================

Who performs this: Platform owner

Use this only when the team wants disposable demonstration data.

1. Open Admin Maintenance.
2. Find Presentation sandbox.
3. Review the current sandbox record counts.
4. Select Clear and reseed sandbox to restore the complete demonstration journey.
5. Confirm every sandbox property displays “Test property”.
6. Select Clear sandbox only when the team wants to remove all sandbox records.

Expected result:

[ ] Exactly one sandbox host is created.
[ ] All sandbox properties carry the Test property badge.
[ ] Live businesses and properties remain unchanged.
[ ] The action appears in platform audit history.
